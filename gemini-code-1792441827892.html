Overview

What is SMORES?

SMORES is an underwater sensing platform designed to collect dissolved oxygen readings at multiple depths in coastal environments. The system consists of cylindrical sensor housings deployed beneath a surface buoy, connected to topside power and data acquisition systems via cabling. The data collected supports ongoing oceanographic research in collaboration with Harvard University.

My Contributions

What I built and fixed

Sensor Calibration

Calibrated 28 optical oxygen sensors prior to deployment, verifying output readings against known reference values to ensure measurement accuracy in the field.

Topside Power & Networking

Set up and managed the topside power system using solar charge controllers, ran network cabling between the buoy and sensor array, and verified all connections before deployment.

Critical Hardware Fix

During the first deployment, the SD card was dislodged by component movement inside the cylindrical housing — causing a critical data loss failure. Diagnosed the root cause back in the lab and designed a custom foam restraint that held all components in place for subsequent deployments.

Successful Redeployment

Following the foam restraint fix, the platform was redeployed successfully. It is currently deployed in a coastal location, actively collecting dissolved oxygen data.

Overview

In-House High-Precision PCB Milling

Trained on specialized LPKF PCB prototyping equipment to fabricate custom circuit boards in support of FIU research initiatives. The workflow covers everything from CAD/Gerber file preparation to hardware isolation milling and physical board contouring.

Technical Steps & Execution

Fabrication Process

CAM & Gerber Import

Imported Gerber and drill files into LPKF CircuitPro, performed design rule checks, and aligned layers for double-sided or single-sided board milling.

Toolpath Optimization

Generated optimized toolpaths for isolation milling, copper rub-out, and mechanical drilling to maximize trace accuracy and minimize tool wear.

Hardware Milling & Drilling

Operated the LPKF ProtoMat S103 high-speed spindle system to mill isolation channels into copper-clad substrates and drill precision via holes.

Quality Assurance

Inspected traces under magnification for short circuits or thin copper bridges, preparing the prototype boards for component soldering and research integration.
